Classroom R6 S1 implementation note
=================================

Scope and release posture
-------------------------
S1 relationship management remains disabled unless CLASSROOM_S1_ENABLED is exactly
1. Its narrow live v1 onboarding path allows Woodshed Site Admin to issue a signed,
expiring Program invitation and the invited adult to accept it even while the S1
management flag is off. Schema installation alone creates no Program or authority.
Existing free Director behavior is unchanged; neither onboarding nor enabling the
flag provides paid entitlement or student reporting.

Woodshed manually approves an exact existing Organization as a Music Program and
sends the intended founding Owner / Head Director an email invitation. Site Admin
sign-in and CSRF/origin checks authorize issuance; issuance creates no adult,
Program, role, relationship, subscription, or session. The signed invitation binds
its purpose, version, exact Organization ID, normalized invited email, and expiry.
Opening the emailed link proves control of that mailbox, but GET alone creates
nothing. A successful POST rechecks the invitation and unprovisioned Organization.
An existing TrustedVerifier must enter its existing PIN; a new email must provide
a display name and valid four-digit PIN to create a TrustedVerifier credential.
The exact authenticated adult becomes canonical Owner and receives the founding
Head Director grant in the same transaction as the Program and audit evidence.
Replay after provisioning refuses. Failed email delivery creates no Program
authority, and deliberate issuance may be retried.

This proof does not establish legal identity, employment, school purchasing or
billing authority, student-data consent, or parent authorization. No Class code,
student enrollment, student relationship, trial, payment, entitlement, teaching
assignment, or reporting capability follows from the invitation. Self-service
school signup and stronger school verification remain optional later work.

The synthetic local test service remains separately guarded. It requires an
already credential-authenticated adult explicitly listed in server-side
CLASSROOM_S1_PROVISIONER_IDS, CLASSROOM_S1_LOCAL_PROVISIONING=1, and an isolated
/tmp/classroom-s1-*/... SQLite file or a literal loopback PostgreSQL URL with a
classroom_s1_ database name. Provisioned addresses must use .test or .invalid,
and Program provisioning verifies those synthetic actor/owner domains. These
checks contain local provisioning; they do not constitute mailbox proof. No
operator, account, or Program is seeded
by the migration or normal startup.

Identity and authentication
---------------------------
Adults remain TrustedVerifier IDs; registered students remain WoodchuckProfile IDs.
The service reuses validate_email, is_valid_pin, hash_pin, and existing
authenticate_trusted_verifier. Existing email always requires its own credentials;
an invitation cannot replace its hash/name or claim its account. A unique normalized
email arbitrates concurrent creation. Accepting with a new email creates only its
credential, Program ownership, explicit founding Head Director role, and audit:
no student connection, profile, billing account, subscription, or consent.

AdultAuthentication is internal opaque evidence minted from current credentials,
bound to its SQLAlchemy Session and outer transaction, with no client actor-ID or
role adapter. Services recheck its credential hash against the database after
Program lock waits. A caller must authenticate again after commit/rollback. A
forged ID, Team Director capability, parent session, or child director emailed-link
session cannot supply this evidence. The live acceptance path uses the existing
adult credential rules and does not turn a matching email or display name into
authentication. Its browser pages and mutation requests require the appropriate
rate limiting, Origin/CSRF, strict inputs, and no-store handling.

Physical relationships and constraints
-------------------------------------
Eight additive tables provide the minimum independently retained records:

* classroom_programs extends Organization by primary key. Its NOT NULL restrictive
  owner FK is the one canonical Owner. Historical created_by_verifier_id and legacy
  organization memberships have no authority. owner_version invalidates proposals
  across successive transfers, including transfer away and back.
* classroom_role_grants preserves explicit head_director, admin, code_manager, and
  billing periods and end evidence. Compatible roles and multiple Programs work.
  Owner is not an independently removable role. Head Director and code-manager
  records represent responsibility only; code services remain S2. Billing cannot
  mark payments or activate access. Only Owner grants/revokes Program roles.
* classroom_classes has a stable ID, Program FK, and display name independent of
  codes. Owner/Admin can create Classes and assign/end teaching; neither role
  implies teaching. Other roles see Program metadata and only Classes they teach.
* classroom_teaching_assignments preserves explicit teaching periods, grant/end
  actors, and revoked/departed reasons. Teachers can record their own departure.
  Teaching grants no practice-verification, reporting, or enrollment capability.
* classroom_student_memberships uniquely anchors (Class, ordinary student ID).
* classroom_membership_periods preserves active/held segments and departed/changed
  endings on that anchor. Administrative hold is distinct from departure. There
  is no runtime insertion/join/import/suspension/reinstatement helper. Tests insert
  only synthetic relationships to demonstrate multiple Classes and period history.
* classroom_ownership_transfers stores the exact initiating Owner, recipient,
  owner version, pending/accepted/cancelled/superseded state, and resolution time.
* classroom_audit_events stores typed action, actor, target, Program/Class scope,
  relevant grant/assignment/proposal and old/new Owner references, server timestamp.
  No free-text payload, credentials, codes, tokens, parent or practice data.

Database constraints enforce referential integrity, non-null canonical Owner,
allowed roles/states/actions, positive periods, end evidence, unique membership
anchors, at most one open role/assignment/membership period, and one pending
transfer per Program. Composite FKs prevent assignment and audit scope substitution.
All new FKs use RESTRICT: hard deletion cannot silently erase authority/history.
A future membership service must lock the anchor and reject overlapping historical
periods; the database's open-period uniqueness is not a general overlap exclusion.
S1 has no service that writes student membership, so it cannot claim that workflow.

Authority operations and transactions
------------------------------------
The caller owns commit/rollback. Every Program authority mutation and its success
audit share a savepoint. Synthetic-only adult identity creation by itself grants
no Program authority and does not fabricate a Program-scoped audit or consumer
billing membership. SQLite explicitly begins a physical transaction before that savepoint,
so RELEASE cannot accidentally commit before the caller's outer rollback. SQLite
writes require foreign_keys=ON; the shared application engine is not changed.
SQLite is sequential local evidence, not a substitute for PostgreSQL row locks.

Management locks the Program first, then scoped Class/relationship/proposal rows.
Adult existence and credential reads are fresh but take no UPDATE row locks; S1
authority operations never mutate credentials. This avoids shared adults causing
cross-Program lock inversions; restrictive FKs retain the identity evidence.
One outer transaction may mutate only one existing Program. Provisioning locks the
preexisting Organization before creating its Program. Synthetic provisioning
requires both an authenticated local provisioner and exact founding adult evidence;
live invitation acceptance requires a valid approved invitation and exact founding
adult credential proof. The founding adult becomes Owner and receives an explicit
Head Director grant only through this deliberate service, never by inference.
Program, role, audit, and any new adult credential commit together or roll back
together. PostgreSQL Organization locking serializes competing acceptances.

The live provisioning audit names the authenticated founding adult as actor and
target. It does not attribute an invitation to a student or invent a
TrustedVerifier for the Site Admin. The current audit schema has no durable
per-Program record naming the issuing Site Admin operator. The protected issuance
route emits sanitized operational success/failure events and request correlation,
without Program, email, or token data. Woodshed must retain its manual approval
evidence separately; a later audit refinement may link that approval to a named
operator and Program.

PostgreSQL writes require READ COMMITTED; stronger snapshot isolation is rejected
rather than allowing an old role snapshot after a lock wait. All authority checks
use refreshed Program state and database role predicates following acquisition. Transfer proposals require the current authenticated
Owner. Acceptance requires the exact recipient's independent credential evidence,
locks/reloads the Program and proposal, and rechecks owner/version/status/recipient.
A newer proposal supersedes the older one with an audit event. Pending transfer
proposals have no token or email mechanism and remain valid only while their owner
version and pending status are current. Founding Program invitations cannot be
used to transfer ownership.
Acceptance atomically replaces the canonical Owner, advances its version, resolves
the proposal and records old/new Owner audit references. Replays fail; no successful
audit survives a failed or rolled-back transfer. Independently granted roles remain
with the outgoing adult; powers derived solely from ownership disappear. Transfer
never duplicates Classes/memberships or grants teaching/reporting to the recipient.

No S1 adult management operation locks PTA, seasons, or student profiles. Existing
student anonymization locks PTA authority -> seasons -> profile and is unchanged.
It retains ordinary profile IDs; no Classroom path reads student data. Future
student enrollment/deletion integration must preserve that existing cross-system
lock order and reconcile Classroom restrictions before any live enrollment.

Dashboard boundary
------------------
director_sections delegates Connected Students to the unchanged dashboard_metrics
function and its existing free relationship/age/privacy calculations. my_classes
returns authorized administrative Program/Class metadata only: no student names,
counts, notes, charts, practice, games, or exports. Paid capabilities and reporting
are explicitly not_implemented. require_student_reporting always denies, even for
Owner/Admin/Teacher and even with a synthetic student membership. Class selection
requires its exact Program; unauthorized substitution fails. The disabled branch
performs no Classroom schema query. Missing schema while enabled raises an actual
schema error, never a legitimate empty roster.

Migration and preservation
--------------------------
The statically verified chain f19arcade001 -> p20team001 -> p21team001 now adds only
c22class001. No previous migration is modified. The compatible calendar and PTA
operators accept exactly one installed revision: p21team001 or c22class001. They
retain their existing calendar/authority structural checks, including required
columns, CHECK constraints, singleton state, partial unique indexes, restrictive
origin-season FKs and exact membership-interval trigger verification where
applicable. For c22, both also validate all eight Classroom tables: required
column types/nullability, primary keys, CHECK constraints, FKs (including composite
scope and RESTRICT), unique constraints and indexes with their partial predicates.
A missing version row, older/unknown revision, multiple heads, or false/incomplete
c22 stamp refuses. There is no skip-validation option or dependency on the
Classroom feature flag. All new tables begin empty; no
legacy row, owner, enrollment, entitlement, trial, sharing grant, cohort, Team,
contest attribution, C001/PILOT-D1, or PTA state is inferred or rewritten.
Downgrade checks every Classroom table first and refuses if any row exists; an
unused schema can be removed. No used authority/history is silently destroyed.

Operator compatibility matrix (compatible operator code required):

Command/workflow                     p21team001           c22class001
ordinary provision_weeks plan/apply  supported            supported
PTA PLAN                             supported            supported
PTA APPLY of newly approved plan     supported            supported
PTA ACTIVATE of exact staged plan    supported            supported
PTA VERIFY of activation receipt     exact-state check    exact-state check
historical team_preflight/repair     refused              refused
team_activate                       retired              retired

Supported does not waive scope, schema, authority, time-boundary, target, hash,
maintenance/backup/writer/finalizer acknowledgments or explicit confirmation.
Calendar apply remains insert-only and pending-boundary writes remain gated.
Historical continuity repair retains its separate d17contest001 restriction;
this patch does not authorize seasonal copying or revive team_activate.

Installed schema and an approved plan's immutable PTA contract are distinct.
New c22 plans report revision=c22class001 and pta_contract_revision=p21team001.
A plan generated and staged on p21 can activate and immediately verify after an
additive c22 migration using its original file, hash, boundary and stored stage,
provided approved authority and all other checks still pass. An activation and
receipt created on p21 can verify after that migration only when every originally
protected evidence row remains exactly unchanged. Old receipts do not claim to
cover Classroom tables; separate before/after preservation checks protect those
tables during compatible operator writes. Unstaged p21 plans presented for APPLY
on c22 refuse with plan_schema_revision_changed before writes: generate a new c22 plan
and obtain fresh approval. Never edit an approved file or hash, clear a stage,
recreate a receipt, or stamp a database to make validation pass.

VERIFY is an exact activation/receipt comparison, not an ongoing health check.
Later calendar weeks, memberships, practice or reward activity can invalidate
its evidence; post_activation_state_changed and other existing refusals remain.
Do not reactivate to repair an old receipt. Empty closing rosters and frozen
legacy history retain their treatment. Positive compatibility rehearsals use
actual migrations and synthetic local data, including populated Classroom rows;
falsified revision stamps are negative tests only.

Operator rollout ordering
-------------------------
Compatible operator code must be available before any staged p21 database is
upgraded to c22. An old pinned binary still rejects c22, including activation and
VERIFY; merging a source fix does not update external operator installations.
Coordinate their separately approved rollout before the schema upgrade so the
due-boundary runtime fence retains a usable activation operator. Do not clear or
move an approved stage to bypass it. This local compatibility patch does not
authorize deployment, public enablement or restoration. The separate live v1
Program invitation mechanism does not authorize paid Classroom capabilities.

Deferred contracts
------------------
Live v1 onboarding uses manually approved Site Admin issuance plus emailed mailbox
proof and adult credentials. The proof deliberately stops short of identity,
employment, purchasing, and student-data authority. A future self-service or
school-verification workflow can be considered separately. This local slice does
not claim deployment or full R6 readiness.
S2: entitlements, trials, packages,
codes/joining, enrollment/hold/departure workflows and account reconciliation.
S3: separately approved student capability/reporting/history authorization; no
speculative consent scopes or administrative-student projection exist here.
S4: Guest Classroom context and local Pristine integration; unchanged in S1.
S5: reconcile restored Owner/role/assignment/membership restrictions and audit
history with the external recovery process, including separately approved retention
and deletion handling. These audit rows are not an external restore-replay ledger;
there are no permanent tombstones, retention buffer, recovery or rollout claims.
R7: full director workflows, games, reflections, reports, exports, purchasing.

Validation commands, baseline failures/retries and final file hashes are recorded
outside the patch in the final response's temporary evidence manifest. No database,
credential, or generated test artifact belongs in the repository patch.
