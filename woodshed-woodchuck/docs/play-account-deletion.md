# Google Play account deletion and Data safety

These are draft deletion-related disclosures for the local Woodshed Woodchuck candidate. Enter them in Play Console only after the public request path and Support fulfillment have been verified and the approved retention treatments are enforced. See the [release-readiness report](play-deletion-release-readiness.md).

## App content → Data safety

| Item | Draft disclosure |
| --- | --- |

| In-app deletion | Signed-in students open **SHOP → Contact → Account & Privacy** and confirm with their Woodchuck ID, four-digit PIN, and `DELETE`. Students on the age and consent screen can use **Account privacy and deletion**, including under-13 students waiting for parental permission. |

| Web request process | Users can email `support@woodshedwoodchuck.com` with the subject `Account Deletion Request` and include the Woodchuck ID if known. This draft covers requests to delete an account and its associated data. A declaration that users can delete data while keeping their accounts needs its own verified process.

## Adult accounts in the Android shell

Inviting a verifier or Band Director displays a clickable acceptance link. When that link uses the configured Android origin, the shell's popup router opens it in the main WebView. Accepting a valid invitation with a name and new PIN creates a `TrustedVerifier` account and the authorized student connection. Classroom Program and parent-authorized director invitations can also create the same adult account when accepted through their authorized flows. The manifest declares only a launcher intent, so links opened from email follow the device's usual browser routing.

The local signed bundle (`woodshed-internal-signed.aab`, `com.woodshedwoodchuck.app` version `0.1.0-r4b`) contains the trusted origin and the same-origin popup behavior in its DEX. Local HTTP tests exercised both adult invitation roles and dashboard links. The package currently in Play and an installed-device journey were outside this local check.

The student and guardian procedures act on the student account. The separate adult executor replaces the verifier's sign-in email, name and PIN, closes the adult's access and preserves the original verifier ID on students' verification history. School ownership, ambiguous private grants and unresolved billing can hold a request. Support records the dependency and verifies its resolution before applying the deletion. Historical contact, linked records and provider copies still require their applicable disposition. [Adult Support procedure](adult-deletion-support.md) · [Approved retention decisions](deletion-retention-decisions.md)

## Subscriptions and Stripe

The candidate Membership screen displays plan prices and says purchasing is unavailable. The native shell bundles no Play Billing client. The web checkout endpoint can use Stripe hosted Checkout if both purchase/provider flags and the Stripe configuration are enabled. The submitted app's actual settings and routes must prevent unapproved external checkout for digital membership; verify the Play policy or authorized program for the release markets. [Google Play Payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10281818?hl=en)

Deletion does not call a billing-provider cancellation or erase all payment records. The public section directs owners of paid memberships to Support for separate cancellation. Verify subscription status, any continuing charges, provider-held information and billing retention for applicable accounts. The code supports hosted card entry and retains subscription and payment information, so reconcile purchase history and any payment data collected or shared against what the submitted build enables. [Google Play Data safety guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en)

Google requires a discoverable in-app path and a public web resource that can initiate a request. It allows a customer-service email and a prominent Privacy Policy anchor. Its policy also requires deletion of associated data, including relevant service-provider action, with clear disclosure of legitimate retention. Account restriction, selected data removal, and a working email link alone provide incomplete evidence for that declaration. [Google Play account-deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en)

Keep linkable records in the Data safety review, including those associated with a pseudonym, and reconcile the declaration with what the submitted app and its controlled WebView actually collect and share. Google lists apps exclusively on the internal testing track as exempt from the Data safety form. Verify the actual track before relying on that exemption. [Google Play Data safety guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en)

Parents can direct deletion of their child's information and refuse its further use or collection. The local Support procedure verifies the parent's relationship to the specific child, and the Privacy Policy describes the parent's choices. [16 CFR § 312.6](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312/section-312.6)

The COPPA Rule also requires the operator to implement a written retention policy and include it in the online notice. The policy must identify the specific collection purposes, business need for retention, and deletion timeframe. The approved policy still needs verified enforcement for the records below. [16 CFR § 312.10](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312/section-312.10)

## Retention evidence and release holds

| Category | Current treatment and policy boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary student data | A newly confirmed deletion clears the ordinary account, state, practice, contact, analytics and access fields in the same transaction. The approved outer limit is 30 calendar days from the original deletion or verified confirmation. For older accounts, use the corresponding prior-cleanup procedure with authentic dates and evidence. A separate receipt records the actual cleanup time. Completion of a population-wide backfill and provider disposal require separate evidence. |
| Unactivated family requests | Unanswered requests expire after 48 hours and verified activation links expire 72 hours after verification. The approved retention limit is 30 days after expiry. Local cleanup eligibility begins 29 days after expiry, or 29 days after an eligible failed verification completes. The cleanup preserves activated verification and consent linkage. Email rate-limit timestamps have a separate one-hour window. Saved evidence qualifies the day-29 code locally. The latest preserved publication snapshot still showed the older scheduled artifact. Successful live runs and provider disposal remain unverified. |
| Activated consent and school authorization | Original consent, notices, school authority and audit links are protected evidence. Minimal consent evidence may be held while authority is active and, only with a documented need, up to 12 months after it ends. Use each grant’s recorded end and disposal dates. The current executors cannot dispose of identifying fields in activated KWS evidence or the signed school receipt. |
| Historical student, team, award and account records | Shared practice and reporting facts, verification actors, Classroom IDs, memberships, contest keys, reward receipts, required age evidence and retired-ID markers can remain linkable. Student cleanup removes closed-invitation contacts, spent digests and eligible delivery history. Adult cleanup can erase the accepting adult’s verified address on a consistently dated invitation. Independently supplied addresses and uncertain historical matches require review. [Eligible and retained fields](play-deletion-release-readiness.md) |
| Operations and backups | Approved ceilings are 30 days from creation for ordinary logs, 90 days after the security event or investigation closes for security or fraud records, and 90 days after case closure for Support correspondence, subject to documented legal obligations. Shorter existing limits still apply. Retention enforcement for the application log host, security systems, mailbox and their provider copies remains unverified. Backups have a 30-day creation-based maximum. [Restore containment](deletion-restore-containment.md) covers isolated copies. |
| Third parties and other accounts | Reconcile information sent to mail, hosting, KWS and any billing provider. Identify subscriptions and processor or legal retention obligations, request deletion where required, and record actual acknowledgments. A student's request does not authorize deleting another person's verifier account, shared award or school evidence. |
| Devices and exports | Browsers and the Android WebView can retain account state, save-conflict copies and tab drafts. After a successful form deletion, the confirmation page removes recognizable account-bound entries from accessible browser storage and signals open same-origin tabs. Local Chrome tests verified removal of the student's saved state, older copy, draft and Wheel action while another student's entries survived. Closed or offline devices, older cached clients, unavailable storage and user exports can retain copies. Support cannot remotely erase them. Give the requester device-specific instructions and explain the shared-device effect of clearing all site or app storage. |

Support records the request date and original clock, verification, confirmed scope, actual outcome, retained exceptions, provider follow-up, and notice to the requester. Escalate overdue or unresolved requests and keep them open. The approved operational targets are acknowledgment within two business days, beginning verification within five, restriction within two business days of verified confirmation, and notice within five business days after verified completion. Mailbox routing, staffing, historical-case reconciliation and these service levels require operational evidence.

The draft Privacy Policy carries the November 17, 2026 candidate date. Set its effective date to the actual publication date if the release occurs later.
