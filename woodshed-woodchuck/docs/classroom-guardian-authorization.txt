Classroom parent/guardian authorization — local implementation
=============================================================

Scope and existing contracts
----------------------------
This continues the accepted D2 41-field individual reporting implementation.
It adds qualified parent choices for an existing child's exact Class. It does
not activate Classroom or KWS, approve new data fields, create parent accounts,
verify a school, send new notifications, or change existing KWS bindings,
callbacks, notices, account permissions, or free director permissions.

The existing native KWS flow records adult verification, a separate guardian
attestation, acceptance of the current account notice and account permission.
Its verified result is activated into an exact child's ConsentEvidence. Parent
access then requires that consent's separate one-use emailed link and bounded
private parent session. Matching an email, a TrustedVerifier role/PIN, payment,
a school domain or a Class membership cannot substitute for that mechanism.

Class membership is a retained administrative relationship. Existing native
enrollment remains separate from permission to participate, report or share.
Protected students may have a confirmed membership while Class learning awaits
qualified parent participation permission. Ordinary account-authorized private
practice, personal access and saving retain their existing sources.

Qualified parent evidence
-------------------------
The new path requires current native parent authentication for the exact child;
active current account consent; exactly one activated KWS verification linked to
that consent; current configured environment/organization/product and notice;
the original immutable pending-request binding; completed adult verification;
original guardian attestation/notice times; matching activation, child and parent
contact bindings; and a fresh separate Class guardian attestation/explicit choice.
The accepted guardian wording is reused. The provider's verification result is
never treated as proof of legal guardianship or as Class reporting consent.

Activated account verification is durable evidence, not an unexpired pending
verification request. It does not depend on the old request's activation deadline
after valid activation. Current account withdrawal, evidence invalidation,
deletion or notice/configuration mismatch still blocks the Class path.

Independent Class choices
------------------------
Participation: the parent expressly authorizes this Class's learning participation
and the already approved private roster identity (school name/WC#/instrument and
membership records) under existing exact Program/Class administrative authority.
It does not create enrollment, reporting, chart sharing or a verifier connection.

Reporting: a separate parent choice names the current exact teaching assignments
and binds the fixed classroom-reporting-41-v1 fields. The student must separately
accept the existing educational reporting notice for the same Class/recipients.
Both current participation and current parent/student reporting choices are
required. Parent reporting is optional and never required for Class learning.

Individual P-Charts: existing S3 and per-chart sharing readers keep their strict
account boundary. Parent comprehensive consent does not enable protected-child
Class P-Chart sharing, create a share, or expand a free DirectorPermission. The
existing separately authorized private director flow retains its own permissions.

Under-13 and all consent-linked reporting remain denied if any required evidence
is missing or invalid. Unlinked eligible 13+ student reporting keeps its accepted
contract. The 41-field reader and rating formula are unchanged. The other 26
fields, comprehensive notes, exports and cohort reporting stay unavailable.

Dates, source changes and withdrawal
-----------------------------------
C34 choices start at explicit server-recorded acceptance, never at enrollment,
KWS verification, a supplied date or an earlier authorization. They expire no
later than the current Program entitlement. Each binds its current membership
period, entitlement, source revision, notice and evidence references. Reporting
additionally binds the participation receipt and exact current recipients.

The student reporting source revision includes both guardian receipts. Rejoining,
membership holds/reinstatement, archive/reactivation, entitlement/source changes,
changed teachers, changed account declaration/consent, withdrawal or replacement
require fresh applicable choices. Reversing a source change does not revive old
receipts. Current parent sessions are rechecked after lock waits; a stale form
cannot undo withdrawal or silently switch child/Class/directors.

Withdrawal of parent reporting immediately stops comprehensive reads without
ending participation, enrollment, account permission or individual chart choices.
Participation withdrawal also blocks Class learning and reporting but retains
membership records and ordinary separately authorized private practice. The
existing account withdrawal invalidates all dependent choices. Reapproval needs
fresh applicable student/parent choices and cannot backfill earlier private data.

Reporting lower bounds include both guardian acceptances and the existing exact
student/teacher/membership/entitlement/account bounds. Complete America/Chicago
occurrence days and independent submission instants must both qualify, including
DST boundaries. Missing/partial measurements stay unavailable. Exact current
practice streak remains unavailable under the accepted complete-day boundary.

Storage, locking and operator boundaries
---------------------------------------
Additive c34guardian001 follows c33identity001 and adds only guardian authorizations,
exact reporting recipients and minimal audit receipts. Restrictive scoped foreign
keys, one-open-choice uniqueness, date/shape checks and retained audit evidence
support the service's current-source checks. The tables contain no copied names,
addresses, credentials, raw KWS results, practice measurements or notes. No earlier
row, schema object, migration or consent receipt is rewritten or backfilled.

Mutation/read authority follows Program -> Class -> existing consent/profile ->
membership order, PostgreSQL READ COMMITTED and caller-owned transactions/savepoints.
Supported writers serialize on those fences. Capability projections remain read
only and never acquire a Program lock beneath an account lock. C33 protected
identity remains masked when its global account-lock prepass is disabled.

The frozen C34 operator contract validates the exact new schema and preserves its
tables in operator snapshots. False old stamps with guardian tables refuse. Used
evidence refuses downgrade. Original PTA receipts and P31-only new-issuance rules
are unchanged; this is not permission to issue a new activation on a later head.

CLASSROOM_GUARDIAN_AUTHORIZATION_ENABLED defaults off and requires S1/S2. Protected
Class benefits fail closed when the workflow is off; disabling its UI cannot
revive withdrawn permission. Participation and its withdrawal do not depend on
S3/reporting gates. Reports still require their separate original reporting gates.
Install/validation and any activation remain separate operations; no production
migration or feature enablement is performed by this local implementation.

Specific missing Leadership decision: school authority
-----------------------------------------------------
No independently verifiable school-authority contract exists in the accepted
sources. School authorization therefore has no positive implementation path.
The service rejects school claims before using them as authority; the UI directs
users to the qualified parent/guardian flow. Director role, school email/domain,
Class enrollment and institutional funding never become school consent evidence.

Leadership must approve the school's authority-evidence contract before a school
path can be implemented: the qualifying institution and authorized signer/delegation;
an independent issuer or verification process and its authenticated evidence;
the exact child/Program/Class, allowed participation/reporting purposes and fixed
field scope; notice/attestation versions; effective dates and retained evidence;
and expiry/revocation verification. No school portal checkbox, unverifiable letter,
administrator assertion or operator-provided boolean is silently accepted instead.

Qualification limits
--------------------
Local tests use synthetic native signed KWS callbacks and existing activation and
parent-access mechanisms on disposable actual migrations. They do not contact
KWS/mail providers or independently establish a real person's legal relationship.
PostgreSQL waits, complete desktop/mobile journeys and a focused independent
security review qualify this change. They are not production/legal/provider or
whole-product activation acceptance. External evidence records exact results.
