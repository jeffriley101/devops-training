Classroom school agreement and individual sharing - local implementation
=======================================================================

This continues accepted guardian checkpoint ab03a431ea2ead669cd0eb4c71ae99a8badcb880.
The earlier C34 document remains a historical description of that checkpoint.
Current policy supersedes its positive under-13 comprehensive-reporting path:
comprehensive director reporting is unavailable under 13, regardless of guardian
or school agreement evidence. Existing records are retained, not rewritten.

One school agreement, two possible approvers
-------------------------------------------
A band director can approve when school-granted authority has been independently
verified. Otherwise an authorized school administrator can approve after the same
verification. Both use the same versioned Program agreement and the same screen.
The approver may be an existing authorized school administrator without a Program
role. Approval does not create a role, Class, membership, entitlement or permission.

The independent evidence screen requires existing site-administrator authentication
AND a fresh PIN for an existing adult reviewer. Reviewer and approver must differ.
An independently sourced, different official school contact confirms the named
approver's authority for the exact Program. A school address, Program role, title
or the approver's own assertion alone cannot create a verified record.

The reviewer records school, approver's existing account, director/administrator
capacity, specific delegated authority, official contact name/position, phone or
verified reply address, official HTTPS directory source, contact time with zone,
and documented call/reply evidence. The confirmation checkbox is unchecked and
requires explicit independent verification. No call or email is made automatically.
Tests use synthetic conversations and never claim real verification took place.

The intended approver signs in with existing credentials, reviews that exact
Program/year and checks the common agreement. No reviewer can accept on behalf of
a different approver. Phone evidence is recorded by a trusted independent reviewer;
the application does not automatically prove a telephone conversation occurred or
that an arbitrary directory URL is authentic. These assurance requirements remain
part of legal review before live use.

Annual period and lifecycle
---------------------------
The authorization year is July 1 through June 30 in the existing America/Chicago
calendar, ending exclusively at July 1 midnight. A midyear agreement is effective
no earlier than its server-recorded acceptance. A next-year agreement can be
recorded early but cannot become effective before July 1. No automatic renewal.

Renewal uses the same form, with explicit annual verification and agreement
acceptance. Prior years remain intact. One unrevoked record per Program/year
prevents competing approvers. Replacement needs explicit revocation and fresh
evidence. The approver, current Program Owner/Admin or authenticated independent
staff can revoke. A teaching assignment alone cannot revoke another approver's
agreement. Expiry, revocation, changed evidence, wrong actor/Program, stale forms
or invalid dates cannot authorize use. Contact evidence remains on the staff
screen; organizational status/authority is visible only within its scoped access.

Legal gate stays closed
-----------------------
The common agreement is classroom-school-agreement-draft-v1. Its version AND
digest have no entry in LEGALLY_APPROVED_AGREEMENTS. School records can be prepared
and accepted locally for review, but authorized_program remains false in shipped
code. No environment variable converts this draft to legal approval. There is no
student-data token, implicit Class activation or automatic historical disclosure.

CLASSROOM_SCHOOL_AUTHORIZATION_ENABLED defaults off and requires S1/S2. Screens:
  /classroom/school/{program_id}       director or named school administrator
  /admin/classroom/school/{program_id} independent staff verification/evidence
The normal Program management screen links to the agreement only when enabled.
All sensitive views are no-store, same-origin, CSRF protected and frame denied.
Every change reauthenticates the current adult and rechecks Program scope after
locks. Forms bind exact record/year/revision, actor and agreement digest.

Student, parent and P-Chart permissions
--------------------------------------
Student enrollment Join and reporting Share choices remain explicit. Parent or
guardian requirements remain separate. School agreement creation/acceptance never
calls enrollment, reporting acceptance or individual chart-selection services.
The existing individual chart invitation path remains distinct from enrollment:
accepted invitations do not silently become a Class membership.

Ordinary eligible 13+ individual chart sharing retains its accepted membership
OR accepted exact invitation source and explicit per-chart recipient choice.
The proposed protected-account adapter uses those same individual boundaries plus
all valid native KWS/account evidence and the exact current parent-authorized named
director chart-view permission and its already accepted native connection. It does
not create or expand that permission or use comprehensive consent as chart consent.
An unrelated teacher, future co-director, matching email, school agreement or general
account consent cannot qualify. Each selection retains its exact native permission
ID. Withdrawal, revocation, disconnect, changed account/recipient/source or a missing
reference denies old selections; a replacement permission cannot revive them.

This new Class use of native named-director permission is also legally unapproved:
LEGALLY_APPROVED_POLICIES has no entry for classroom-named-guardian-pchart-draft-v1.
CLASSROOM_GUARDIAN_PCHART_SHARING_ENABLED defaults off and cannot bypass that gate.
Tests simulate reviewed legal acceptance only inside isolated synthetic fixtures.
Live protected sharing remains unavailable pending that review; existing native
private-director access continues under its original independent permission.

The private practice page now provides the same explicit Send this P-Chart to my
Band Director choice and current recipient selection through the existing sharing
API, alongside the existing P-Book interface. Nothing is checked by default for an
unshared chart. An expressly selected individual historical chart can be viewed
within applicable consent, like the existing individual flow. No other chart or
comprehensive history is disclosed. The Director Dashboard checks this source
independently of masked administrative identity and labels its partial chart view.

Comprehensive reporting retains the exact approved 41 fields and 26 exclusions.
Its occurrence/submission bounds, unavailable values, rating curve and current
streak limitation remain unchanged. The under-13 denial applies before practice
queries even to pre-existing reporting receipts. Parent/consent-linked 13+ reporting
still needs all applicable qualified parent and student authorization.

Storage and concurrency
-----------------------
c35school001 adds one school receipt table and one nullable FK on existing chart
selections. Prior chart choices retain NULL; no consent, roles, relationships,
student data or historical sharing is backfilled. The school row itself retains
reviewer, approver, dates, agreement/evidence digests and revocation provenance;
no second approval ledger or new parent identity is introduced. Used evidence
refuses downgrade. Frozen C35 operator validation/snapshots preserve both additions.

Program-first READ COMMITTED transactions serialize school verify/accept/revoke.
Individual readers prelock explicitly selected subjects in global profile order
before per-chart validation. This prevents overlapping Programs with opposite
chart order from deadlocking. Workspace subject reads remain restricted to the
exact requested membership and their already prelocked account. Native parent
revocation uses the existing consent fence and is rechecked after waits.

Required legal review before any live authorization
---------------------------------------------------
Review and approve the common agreement, permitted school authority/delegation and
Program purposes, official-contact and approver identity assurance, phone/reply
documentation standard, effective-year rules, evidence access/retention and
revocation/renewal obligations. School agreement must not replace individual
student choices or required parent consent in this implementation.

Separately decide whether the existing exact named-director parent notice/permission
covers the proposed Class surface, authentication and explicitly selected historical
P-Charts across ages. If a fresh Class-specific parent notice/receipt is required,
implement that reviewed scope before enabling this adapter. Do not infer legal
coverage from a passing test, school email, director role or KWS adult verification.

No production migration, deployment, push or feature activation is part of this
local implementation. Actual provider calls, real school verification and whole-
product/legal/production acceptance are not asserted by local synthetic tests.
