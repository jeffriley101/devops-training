# Supervised school disposition

## Initial pilot procedure

One designated human administrator handles routine internal cases for the configured pilot Program. The administrator makes two separate signed decisions. One approves a verified database and operating generation. The other approves a particular disposition. The database witness and read-only verifier are technical identities. AI departmental roles cannot supply school acceptance, consent or a human signature.

1. Confirm the requester through an established channel. Obtain applicable school, district, guardian and student authority. For a correction or return, verify the school instruction and the recipient or correction scope, then record administrator approval. For an eligible private roster name, the authorized school actor uses the existing scoped roster correction with its current version. Escalate an unsupported correction.
2. Enter the exact Program, records, original trigger, purpose end and deadline on the restricted control sheet. Apply D30, A12 or S90 under the governing policy. Record any inspection, legal hold or specifically approved exception. Keep a blocked case open.
3. Confirm current destination approval, generation and recovery status. Publish the case under the Program lock and run preview. Check the exact rows, dependencies, exclusions, current eligibility and fingerprint.
4. Add the reviewed fingerprint to the final case. Sign those exact bytes with the designated administrator key. Publish the detached signature under the Program lock.
5. Invoke the fixed execute command through the restricted service. The service rechecks the signed case and privileges, locks the transaction and obtains the required database witness.
6. Confirm the result from a fresh read-only verifier connection. An interruption after commit leaves the case open. Follow the reconciliation steps below before taking further action.
7. Enumerate and remove unnecessary working copies. Verify what remains by independent file inspection. Record verified scope, counts, outcome, original dates and outstanding actions. Close only the verified scope.
