# Approved account-deletion retention decisions

Karen and Phil approved these treatments for identifiable records in the existing inventory, including the Classroom clarification below. The ordinary 30-day clock starts at the original verified confirmation or deletion. Use authentic original dates for older accounts. Record missing evidence as an open case with an owner.

| Identifiable records | Approved minimum treatment and deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Practice and private activity. `practice_charts.profile_id`, dates, durations and submission keys, `practice_chart_verifications.verifier_id`, identifying review text, delivery history, and account-linked Arcade, Weekly, trivia, quest and inventory events. | **Delete** the requester’s private activity within 30 days. Where another student's chart needs its verification, preserve the actual outcome and historical actor reference. Remove the connection back to the adult's identity within 30 days unless a specific school or consent exception below applies. |
| Team, contest and award history. Membership and join-request actor IDs, roster snapshots, `contest_results.profile_id` and `subject_key`, reward source keys, crown and private score history, identifying team-report text. | **De-identify** necessary shared score and eligibility facts within 30 days, removing alternate joins and suppressing identifying combinations. **Delete** individual-only scores and unnecessary identifying text by the same deadline. Preserve teammates' awards and the unowned PTA issuance receipt. |
| Classroom records. Roster, enrollment, teaching and reporting IDs, recipient and audit actor IDs, `organizations.created_by_verifier_id`, school approver and official contact details, source and verification notes. | Revoke the requester’s personal credentials, sessions and student access immediately on verified confirmation. Preserve independently authorized school permissions and other users’ records. Record the specific grant, genuine expiry, withdrawal or replacement, its source, and the resulting disposal dates. An open-ended grant requires review and affirmative renewal within 12 months. **Delete** unnecessary requester contact and free text within 30 days of the actual authority end. **Retain** minimal actor, scope, decision and date only for documented accountability, through a dated deadline at most 12 months after that end. Any school-directed exception requires the school’s applicable written deadline. |
| Family, verification and invitation history. Activated consent/KWS bindings, parent/director contacts and names, invitation addresses and tokens, saved practice recipient contacts, connection IDs, retained declaration and withdrawal evidence. | **Delete** spent credentials, duplicates and unnecessary invitation contacts within 30 days. Review student-owned recipient presets for independently supplied addresses before removing requester data. Preserve a working withdrawal mechanism while a guardian can exercise it. **Retain** only indispensable proof of notice, authority, scope and withdrawal while authority is active and, with a documented reason, up to 12 months after its recorded end. Record withdrawal, replacement or closure as applicable. Keep the shorter existing expiry for unactivated requests. |
| Account and security links. Student and adult tombstone IDs that still join to identifiable records, retired login markers, `account_privacy_rules` age/history, `account_age_corrections` case and actor identifiers. | **Delete** unnecessary age and profile history within 30 days. **Retain** only the minimum identifier needed to deny stale credentials or prevent reattachment through a still-usable backup, until the later of the last applicable credential expiry or seven days after the last capable backup expires. Use the approved 30-day backup maximum from creation to assign a calendar deadline. For a documented security investigation, apply the existing 90-day limit after the event or closure. Demonstrate that disposing of a marker cannot re-enable an old identity. |
| Billing and providers. Payer and seat links, invitation addresses, provider customer/subscription IDs, checkout references, payment effects and identifiable audit metadata. | **Delete** unnecessary account/contact links within 30 days and request corresponding processor deletion. For an outstanding charge or dispute, document the settlement or provider-dispute deadline and delete case-only evidence within 30 days of closure. If a receipt is required for tax, assign its applicable statutory date. Three years after the relevant return was filed is the ordinary U.S. federal example. Verify exceptions with Finance. Keep child identifiers out of tax evidence wherever possible. [IRS recordkeeping guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records) |

Approve de-identification only after checking whether known rosters, retained contacts, dates, aliases and cross-table keys can still identify the person. For a required or protected join, document the specific dependency and approved treatment before changing it. Ordinary logs, Support correspondence, unactivated KWS requests and backups already have policy limits. Those need operational evidence of timely disposal. The source inventory is the November 15 protected-field decisions and November 17 account-deletion review. [COPPA retention rule](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312/section-312.10)

For a Classroom case, record verified confirmation and personal access revocation separately from the school's grant. An authorized owner can record personal role revocation or teaching departure through Classroom management. For the annual school agreement, use the first actual end among its recorded expiry and an authorized withdrawal, revocation or replacement. Enter the grant reference, source, event date, responsible operator, and resulting calendar deadlines in the restricted case. For an open-ended grant, set its review date from the documented grant or last affirmative renewal. At each deadline the assigned operator records the actual disposal and independently checked result, or escalates the overdue case. Missing authority evidence and missed deadlines keep the case open. The current Support executor cannot dispose of the school receipt or retire a sole owner before a successor accepts. [Adult Support procedure](adult-deletion-support.md)
