Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Request account deletion

About Woodshed Woodchuck

Woodshed Woodchuck LLC operates Woodshed Woodchuck, a music-practice application for students, families, and music educators.

Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Woodshed Woodchuck account and associated personal information, email support@woodshedwoodchuck.com with the subject Account Deletion Request. You can send the request without signing in or reinstalling the app. Include the Woodchuck ID if you know it. Keep your PIN and sensitive documents out of the initial email.

A parent or legal guardian can use the same email address to make a request for their child. The child's PIN is unnecessary. Support will explain how to verify your identity and authority for the account before processing the request.

Trusted adult account deletion

Verifiers and directors can request deletion of their own account by emailing support@woodshedwoodchuck.com. Include the email address you use to sign in. Keep student information and PINs out of the request. Support verifies that the account is yours and asks you to confirm deletion. If you own a school Program, Support will guide you through transferring ownership to an authorized successor.

If you own a paid membership, ask Support about canceling it. Paid memberships require separate cancellation. Once your account deletion is applied, your former sign-in, PIN and director sessions no longer give access. Students keep their practice charts and verification history. Support can remove your delivery address from a past invitation when its acceptance record matches your verified account and mailbox. Read which records may remain.

Student account deletion

Signed-in students, including those under 13, can also open Account & Privacy and enter their Woodchuck ID, current four-digit PIN, and the word DELETE.

Deletion disables sign-in, changes the public display name to “Deleted Woodchuck,” and ends the student's current team membership and private connections. We clear saved preferences, practice notes and checklist details, remove practice-email presets and account-activity analytics records, and revoke pending verifier invitations. We remove delivery addresses and old link credentials from your closed invitations when their records show a consistent outcome. Where the deletion process has the necessary access, it also removes private game history, delivery logs, private charts and Weekly plans without a shared use, and age declarations that are no longer needed for consent, school or scoring records. Read which records may remain.

Information we collect

A Woodshed account may store a chosen display name, Woodchuck ID, hashed PIN, instrument, experience level, practice goal, age category, account settings, practice dates and durations, practice notes and details, progress, rewards, game activity, contest and team information, and account-access history.

Woodshed does not require a child's legal name to create a Woodchuck identity.

Trusted adult accounts store a name, email address, hashed verifier PIN, and authorized connections to students or school programs.

Guest use

Guest tools work without an account. Guest setup, tempo, chart, and game score are held only in the open page's memory and clear when you reload, leave, or discard them. Woodshed does not save Guest practice history or progress. Guest activity and preferences do not transfer into an account. An account can save future activity after account creation.

The tuner processes microphone audio on your device. Woodshed does not record, upload, transcribe, or save that audio.

Share, Download, and Copy happen only when you choose them. Woodshed does not receive or retain the exported P-Chart. Share may send the chart to an app or service you select. Download saves a local file. Copy writes to your clipboard. Clearing Guest data does not remove content you have already shared, downloaded, or copied.

Guest use can involve signed session cookies, session-security state, abuse-prevention identifiers and counters, and operational or security logs. These do not store Guest practice or game history and do not create a Guest analytics profile.

A Pre-Beta invitation or recognized Pre-Beta Secret Symbol can store a fixed registration-source marker in the signed session cookie. The marker may survive navigation and reload and remain during the session cookie's lifetime. Successful Guest discard clears the marker from the cookie. The source may transfer into enrollment or consent records when you register or begin the parent-authorization process. The marker does not create a Guest identity or grant account access, Full Access, or parent or guardian consent.

Children under 13 and parent permission

Before an under-13 account is activated for private saved practice, Woodshed asks a parent or legal guardian to review the parent notice, choose the requested permissions, and complete adult verification.

Woodshed uses Epic Kids Web Services (KWS) for adult verification. KWS receives the parent's email address, the configured country or region and language, and an opaque verification reference. Verification may involve payment-card verification handled by KWS. Woodshed does not receive the parent's card number or card details.

Adult verification by itself does not grant permission. The parent or legal guardian must also affirm their authority and explicitly accept the selected Woodshed permissions.

How we use information

We use account and activity information to operate Woodshed, save and restore progress, provide practice tools and analytics, administer rewards and contests, manage account access, support parent and educator features, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and understand how the service is being used.

Sharing and public activity

Some eligible users may have chosen display names, team information, qualifying practice results, achievements, or contest results shown to other Woodshed users.

Under-13 individual results are excluded from public and other-student standings. Earlier private history does not become public merely because a student later becomes eligible for ordinary public participation.

A parent may separately authorize sharing with a specifically named music director. If authorized, that director may receive the student's identity, instrument, practice charts, dates, durations, notes or details, and basic practice metrics within the permission granted by the parent. Chart-review permission is a separate choice.

Service providers

Woodshed uses service providers to operate the application. These currently include Render for application and database hosting, Google services for Woodshed email delivery and support, and Epic Kids Web Services for adult verification. These providers receive information necessary to perform their services.

For purchases made through Stripe, Stripe processes payment details and Woodshed receives information about the subscription and payments.

Parent access and choices

A verified parent or legal guardian may request access to information associated with their child, request correction or deletion, withdraw account permission, stop further collection or use, and withdraw separately authorized director access.

Parent access is tied to the recorded permission relationship and secure emailed access links. Knowing an email address, owning a membership, or knowing a student PIN does not by itself provide access to a child's private information.

Retention and deletion

We retain information while it is reasonably needed to operate the requested Woodshed features, maintain account and consent records, protect security and service integrity, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Unanswered parent-permission requests expire after 48 hours. A verified Woodshed activation link expires 72 hours after verification. When permission is never granted, our policy limits retention of unnecessary parent contact information and related request records to 30 days after the request expires. Our policy allows us to keep the minimum evidence of activated consent while authorization is in effect and, where there is a documented need, for up to 12 months after authorization ends.

Deleting an adult sign-in leaves independently authorized school permissions in place. Our policy requires review and renewal of open-ended school permissions within 12 months. When a school permission ends, our deadline for removing unnecessary contact details and notes is 30 days. We may keep a minimal record of who authorized what and when for a documented accountability need, up to 12 months after the permission ends. A school-directed exception requires a written deadline.

Our retention policy sets limits of 30 days from creation for ordinary application logs, 90 days from a security event or the end of its investigation for security or fraud records, and 90 days after a Support case closes for correspondence. Specific legal obligations may require longer retention of some records.

Our policy sets a 30-day limit from verified confirmation or deletion to remove unnecessary linked data. Some records can still remain linked to a deleted account past that limit while a case is unresolved. These can include practice dates and totals, team and contest results, awards, school or Classroom records, and contact details on historical adult invitations. We may also retain limited account and security records to prevent abuse, billing records to meet legal obligations, and consent and audit records to document permissions and withdrawals. Changing a public display name leaves those record links intact.

A backup made before deletion can contain a copy of your information. Our backup policy sets a maximum of 30 days from creation. Our deadline for retiring an identifier used to block old access is the later of the last applicable credential expiry or seven days after the last backup capable of restoring that identity expires.

For an outstanding charge or dispute, our policy allows 30 days after closure to remove information kept for that case. A required tax receipt has its own statutory retention deadline. Contact Support to ask what remains for your account, why it is kept, or when it is due for deletion.

A browser or the Android app can store a local copy of saved progress or a practice draft. If you use the deletion form, the confirmation page asks that browser to remove stored copies it can identify as yours. A request made through Support cannot remotely erase device storage. Clearing Woodshed site data in a browser or app storage in Android removes local copies on that device and can also clear other Woodshed users' local data on a shared device. Files you downloaded or shared are separate copies.

Security

Woodshed uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to limit unauthorized access to account and child information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Woodshed changes. If a material change affects information collected from a child or permissions previously granted by a parent, Woodshed will obtain any new notice or permission required before relying on the changed practice.
